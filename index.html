About us

A 100 bed hospital, a unit of EHA

Christian Hospital Chhatarpur is a 100 bed hospital providing comprehensive and compassionate healthcare to the people of Chhatarpur and the surrounding Bundelkhand region. It is a unit of Emmanuel Hospital Association, New Delhi.

Our missionTo deliver comprehensive healthcare services to underserved populations, build local capacity through education and training, and advocate for systemic changes that promote health equity for all.
Our visionA world where every individual has access to compassionate, quality healthcare, and where communities are empowered to take control of their health and wellbeing.

FAQ

Questions patients often ask

What are the OPD timings?

The OPD is open Monday to Friday from 08:00 am to 2:30 pm, and on Saturday from 08:00 am to 11:30 am. On Sundays only emergency services are available.

Is the emergency department open 24 hours?

Yes. Emergency and casualty services, with ambulance support, are open 24 hours a day, every day. Call 07682-244311.

Where is the hospital?

Christian Hospital Chhatarpur, Mahoba Road, Dist: Chhatarpur, Madhya Pradesh 471 001.

Does the hospital have an ICU and NICU?

Yes. The hospital has an Intensive Care Unit and a Neonatal ICU, cared for by our nursing team, with senior nursing supervision on evening and night shifts.

How do I apply to the School of Nursing?

The School offers the 3-year GNM programme with an approved intake of 30 students. Call the School office on 07682-249317 or 7869965406 for admission details.

How can I contact the hospital?

Email chhatarpur@eha-health.org, or call the emergency line on 07682-244311.
