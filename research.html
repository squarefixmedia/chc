Featured study

Can Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) be successfully treated at the community level?

This study examines the effectiveness of community-based approaches to treating severe acute malnutrition in children, bringing nutrition education and rehabilitation directly to families in their communities.

Published in partnership with Azim Premji University Health Worker Platform
