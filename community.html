Community health and development

Nearly 50 years of transforming communities through integrated health, livelihoods, and sustainable development programs.

44active projects
Empowering communities since 1976

For nearly five decades, our Community Health Department has worked alongside rural and underserved populations, addressing not just healthcare needs but the underlying social and economic factors that influence wellbeing.

Through 44 active community health and development projects, we combine preventive healthcare with livelihood support, education, and capacity building—creating sustainable change that empowers communities to thrive.

Our approach

Participatory, integrated, and sustainable

Community ParticipationWe work with communities, not for them. Local participation ensures programs are culturally appropriate and sustainable.
Integrated DevelopmentAddressing health alongside economic and social factors creates comprehensive solutions that tackle root causes of poverty and illness.
Capacity BuildingTraining community health workers, local leaders, and groups empowers communities to sustain programs independently.
